anyway let me try to explain the dimensions a little
* 28mm track height: a solid couple mm wider than the width of a us quarter
* 5mm bottom support: arbitrarily defined as approx one quarter the size of a us dime, or the way i visualized it for some reason, half the distance to the center of mass 💀. anyway 5mm became the number of the day because i wanted things to be symmetrical and pretty so anywhere else you see it is just arbitrary
* 22mm max hole height: the size of a nickel and then some to give it some room to fall in while giving the quarter as much room as possible to roll
* 32mm hole width at full size: arbitrarily defined as approx 1.5 times the width of a us nickel
* idk why i made the big triangle leading up to it i guess i thought maybe the coins would behave better if i hit em with the boiling frog instead of a normal hole like every other thingiverse model
* i thought 100mm would be a fun number to make the total track length but then i added 5mm so it wouldnt collapse
* also the 20mm of the triangle that is filled in ensures it doesnt collapse but 20mm was completely arbitrary
